Vendor Risk Discovery Guide

Ask the questions, tick what you hear, and this guide sets the maturity stage and builds the vendor list and command for the Vendor Risk Program Review.

How to use this in the call

The guide has three conversations and one output. Plan on 30 to 40 minutes in total, or split it across two calls.

  1. Maturity (about 15 minutes). Nine questions. Ask them open, then tick the answer that best matches what you hear. The stage is calculated as you go.
  2. Prospect (about 5 minutes). Company, drivers and the people involved.
  3. Critical vendors (about 15 minutes). Capture the ten vendors that matter most. We only need domains to run the report, but the extra context makes the recommendations sharper.
  4. Output. A vendors.txt, the maturity stage and a ready-to-run command.
Tip. Do not read the answer options out loud. Ask the question, listen, then tick. Ask for a best estimate when a percentage is requested. If the answer sits between two options, pick the lower one and add a note.
Do not know. If the prospect cannot answer, tick "Don't know". It is left out of the score and listed as a discovery gap in the notes, which is often a finding in itself.
Privacy. Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Answers are saved locally so a refresh will not lose them. Use Reset between prospects.

Their ten most critical vendors

"If one of your vendors had a serious cyber incident tomorrow, which ten would hurt you most?"

Listen for: access to sensitive or regulated data, operational dependency, revenue impact, concentration (cloud, payments, core platforms) and regulatory outsourcing registers. Aim for a mix, not ten cloud providers.

Only the domain is needed to run the report. Use the vendor's main corporate domain (for example hilton.com, not a product URL). If they cannot name ten, capture what you have and ask them to send the rest.
